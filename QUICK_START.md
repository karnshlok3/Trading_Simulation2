## User Guide

### Trading Interface

#### 1. Market View
- Browse available stocks (AAPL, GOOGL, MSFT, etc.)
- View current prices and price changes
- Click on any asset to select it for trading
- Prices update automatically every 30 seconds

#### 2. Placing a Trade

**To BUY:**
1. Select an asset from the market list
2. Click the "Buy" button in the trade panel
3. Enter the quantity (number of shares)
4. Review the total cost
5. Click "Execute Buy Order"

**To SELL:**
1. Select an asset you currently own
2. Click the "Sell" button in the trade panel
3. Enter the quantity to sell
4. Review the total revenue and estimated P/L
5. Click "Execute Sell Order"

#### 3. Portfolio
View your current holdings:
- Total invested amount
- Current value of holdings
- Unrealized profit/loss
- Return percentage
- Detailed breakdown by asset

#### 4. Trade History
See all your completed trades:
- Trade ID for reference
- Buy/Sell type
- Asset name
- Quantity and price
- Profit/Loss (for sells)
- Timestamp

#### 5. Analytics
Analyze your trading performance:
- Total trades executed
- Win rate (profitable vs loss trades)
- Total profit and net P/L
- Trade distribution charts
- Asset trading frequency

## Trading Tips (Educational)

### Basic Concepts

**1. Buy Low, Sell High**
- Purchase when prices are lower
- Sell when prices increase
- Monitor price changes regularly

**2. Average Buy Price**
- When you buy the same asset multiple times, the system calculates your average purchase price
- This helps you track your cost basis

**3. Profit/Loss Calculation**
- **Unrealized P/L**: The potential profit/loss on assets you still hold
- **Realized P/L**: The actual profit/loss from completed sell trades
- Formula: (Sell Price - Buy Price) × Quantity

**4. Portfolio Diversification**
- Don't invest all money in one asset
- Spread investments across multiple stocks
- Reduces risk of total loss

**5. Trade History**
- Always review your past trades
- Learn from profitable and unprofitable trades
- Track patterns in your trading behavior

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**1. "Insufficient balance" error**
- You don't have enough virtual money
- Check your balance in the header
- Reduce quantity or sell some assets first

**2. "Insufficient shares" error**
- You're trying to sell more than you own
- Check your portfolio holdings
- Reduce the sell quantity

**3. "Trading is disabled" error**
- Admin has paused trading
- Contact admin or wait for trading to resume
- No trades can be executed when disabled

**4. Can't see my trades**
- Make sure you're logged in as the correct user
- Trades only show for the logged-in user
- Check the Trade History tab

**5. Prices not updating**
- Prices update every 30 seconds automatically
- Wait a moment for the next update
- Refresh the page if needed

**6. Lost my data**
- Did you clear browser cache/cookies?
- Data is stored locally in your browser
- No way to recover cleared data
